## Evidence and explanation

The original model contains 1,213 atoms and 1,232 explicit bonds. Fe–His connections are imposed starting-model connections. Camera motion does not change the source coordinates and is not molecular dynamics or observed self-assembly. The separate five-second atomic replay retains its authentic saved frames and captions. Physical simulation time remains unverified.

The reagent insets use PubChem reference conformers: hydrogen peroxide CID 784 and beta-D-glucose CID 64689. Their source URLs, counts and hashes are in assets/assay/reagent-provenance.json. They illustrate reagent identities, not a measured binding trajectory. Green denotes the optical reporter signal. GOx supplies glucose recognition; bundle activity and calibrated reporting still require experiments. No performance curve or detection limit is invented.
